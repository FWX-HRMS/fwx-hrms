Sourcing Candidates

Search public profile pages for people who match a role's required skills.

This searches public profile pages only (via search engine indexing) — it does not scrape private CV databases, does not target Facebook or other platforms where content is private-by-default, and does not look up phone numbers or emails elsewhere. Contact details are only shown when they already appear in the public listing itself (rare) — otherwise it's marked "not public." Review every result manually before contacting anyone, and don't store personal data beyond what screening actually needs.
